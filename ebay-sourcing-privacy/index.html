Privacy Policy: eBay Sourcing App

Irox Technologies. Effective November 9, 2026.

1. Who we are

The eBay Sourcing App (the "app") is software made and run by Irox Technologies ("we", "us"). It is an internal tool that we use to manage our own eBay store. It is not offered to the public, and no one other than the store's owner signs in to it.

2. What the app does

The app finds products from our suppliers, compares their prices with listings on eBay, prepares and publishes the store's own eBay listings, and keeps those listings in line with the supplier's price and stock. While the app's test mode is on, no new listing is published until the owner approves it. Changes to listings already published (price, quantity, taking a listing down and putting it back up) are made by the app after its checks. With test mode off, listings that pass every check are published without asking the owner first.

3. Information the app receives from eBay

  • Our own eBay seller account. When the owner connects the store through eBay's sign in page, eBay gives the app an access token and a refresh token. The app keeps those tokens, the store's eBay username, and the store's business policy and item location settings, so it can create and update the store's listings. The tokens are kept in a file on the owner's computer that only the owner's user account can read.
  • Our own orders. To avoid selling more than the supplier has, the app reads which items in the store's open orders have not shipped yet, and how many of each. eBay's reply includes the whole order, but the app uses only the item, the quantity and whether the order was cancelled. It does not save, display or use buyers' names, addresses, contact details or any other buyer information.
  • Public eBay listings. To compare prices, the app reads public listing details from eBay search results, such as the title, price, shipping cost, item specifics, category, number sold and the listing's web address. The app saves these public listing details, including each listing's item number and web address, but it does not record who the seller is: it does not save the usernames or user IDs of the sellers behind those listings, or any other personal information about other eBay users. A seller's username is used only in memory while a search runs, to leave our own listings out of the comparison.

4. Information from our suppliers

The app reads product details, prices, stock levels and shipping costs from our suppliers, CJdropshipping and Wholesale2B, through the systems those suppliers provide and under their terms.

5. AI service

The app uses Anthropic's Claude service, through the owner's own Anthropic account and under its terms, to read supplier product details, to draft the store's listings, and to give an independent second check before anything is listed, repriced or raised in quantity. For this it sends product and listing details, such as titles, specifications, prices and the proposed listing. No buyer information and no personal information about other eBay users is included. When the app reaches Claude through Claude Code on the owner's computer, Claude Code is told not to keep a copy of these requests.

6. Where information is kept, and for how long

Everything the app keeps is on the owner's computer, not on a public server: its database, its settings file, its log files, a copy of the supplier's product file, private files holding the eBay and supplier tokens, and the owner's file of API keys. If a store website is ever used as a source, its sign in session and checkout screenshots are kept there too. The app's dashboard runs only on that computer. Apart from removing information it should never have kept (such as seller usernames saved by an earlier version), the app does not delete these records by itself; they stay on the owner's computer until the owner deletes them.

7. Sharing

We do not sell information. We share it only as described above: with eBay, to run the store's listings; with our suppliers, to read their products; and with Anthropic, as described in section 5.

8. Buyers

The app does not place supplier orders, and it does not save, display or use buyers' names, addresses or contact details. Information that buyers give eBay is handled by eBay under eBay's own User Privacy Notice.

9. eBay account deletion

We receive eBay's notices when an eBay user asks for their account to be deleted, at a small page hosted for us by Netlify. Because the app keeps no personal information about other eBay users, it holds nothing about them that needs to be deleted, and nothing from the notices is kept. If our own eBay account is closed, the owner deletes the app's eBay tokens from the owner's computer.

10. Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
